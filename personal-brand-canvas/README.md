# Personal Brand Canvas — an AI-guided branding interview

A Kiro skill that interviews you block-by-block with **probing questions** and synthesizes a
one-page **Personal Brand Canvas** — the person-scale analog of the Business Model Canvas
(positioning statement, tagline, content pillars, next actions).

Accompanies a forthcoming post on **[schristoph.online](https://schristoph.online/blog/)**.

## Contents

- `skill/SKILL.md` — the skill definition (interview workflow + constraints)
- `skill/references/question-bank.md` — the per-block probing questions + follow-up ladders
- `skill/references/canvas-template.md` — the output canvas structure
- `example-nadia-brand-canvas.md` — a fully worked example for **Nadia**, a *fictional* persona
  (early-career, youth-employability charity, interested in the future of work + AI)

## The 9 blocks

Purpose & Values · Vision & Goals · Audience · Unique Value · Strengths & Superpowers ·
Passions & Interests · Story & Proof · Personality & Voice · Channels & Content Pillars

> All sample data is fictional — Nadia is a persona, not a real person.
