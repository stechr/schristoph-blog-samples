# Personal Brand Canvas — What Good Looks Like

The difference between a useless canvas and a powerful one is entirely **specificity**. A strong
entry is:

- **Specific** — names real things (people, places, numbers, moments), not categories.
- **Complete** — a full thought, not a fragment or a keyword.
- **Distinctive** — could only describe *this* person. If you could copy it onto someone else's
  canvas, it's too weak.

Per block: what it captures, what a strong entry does, and a weak → strong example.

## 1 · Purpose & Values
- **Captures:** why you do this, and the values you won't trade.
- **Strong entry:** a concrete "so that…" outcome + 2–3 named values, each with a one-line stake.
- ❌ "I want to help people and stay authentic."
- ✅ "So no young person is locked out of the AI economy for lack of a network. Values: **access** (opportunity ≠ postcode), **honesty** (no hype), **momentum** (one real step beats a five-year plan)."

## 2 · Vision & Goals
- **Captures:** near-term (12-mo) and longer (3-yr) direction; the change you create.
- **Strong entry:** a specific position you'll hold + a near, almost-measurable step.
- ❌ "Grow my influence and become a thought leader."
- ✅ "12-mo: a recognised voice on AI + first jobs, publishing weekly. 3-yr: the translator between alarming AI-and-jobs headlines and what a 19-year-old should do on Monday."

## 3 · Audience
- **Captures:** who you serve — specifically — and who it's *not* for.
- **Strong entry:** a describable person + their situation + an explicit exclusion.
- ❌ "Professionals interested in technology and careers."
- ✅ "17–24-year-olds entering work without a professional network, and the youth-workers who guide them. **Not for** senior AI strategists or execs."

## 4 · Unique Value
- **Captures:** your "only-ness" — the intersection few others occupy.
- **Strong entry:** name the 2–3 things you combine that rarely sit together.
- ❌ "I bring a unique perspective and a lot of passion."
- ✅ "In the room with 19-year-olds every week (frontline) + can read a business case (BSc) + genuine AI-and-early-careers fluency. Most 'future of work' voices have at most one of the three."

## 5 · Strengths & Superpowers
- **Captures:** what people repeatedly come to *you* for.
- **Strong entry:** 2–3 concrete capabilities, ideally with a hint of proof.
- ❌ "Communication, leadership, problem-solving."
- ✅ "Turning intimidating jargon into do-it-Monday steps; designing a workshop a nervous teenager actually enjoys; calling which 'AI will take your job' headlines are noise."

## 6 · Passions & Interests
- **Captures:** your energy source, and your emotional stance (emotion = content fuel).
- **Strong entry:** a driving question + an honest emotion.
- ❌ "I'm passionate about technology and helping others."
- ✅ "The question that won't leave me: *what's a genuinely good first job in 2030?* Angry at doom-scrolling that paralyses kids; hopeful about AI as a leveller — *if* access is fixed."

## 7 · Story & Proof
- **Captures:** your arc + evidence you can actually do this.
- **Strong entry:** a turning point + at least one concrete proof (role, result, artifact).
- **Cross-check:** this is where blocks 4 (Unique Value) & 5 (Strengths) earn their claims — every asserted strength or unique value must trace to a proof here. Prefer **external** evidence (results, what people actually said, artifacts) over self-assertion; the brand is how *others* experience you, not how you describe yourself.
- ❌ "I've always cared about this space."
- ✅ "First in my family to finish university; a placement year showed me how much a foot-in-the-door comes down to luck. Now I run my charity's digital-skills programme — including a 6-week pilot that placed its first cohort."

## 8 · Personality & Voice
- **Captures:** how you show up — 3–5 adjectives + tone.
- **Strong entry:** adjectives + a one-line "the [person] who…" persona.
- ❌ "Professional, friendly, knowledgeable."
- ✅ "Warm, straight-talking, hopeful, a little irreverent — plain English, zero jargon; the friend who explains it without making you feel behind."

## 9 · Channels & Content Pillars
- **Captures:** where you show up + the 3–4 themes you'll own.
- **Strong entry:** named channels + 3–4 pillars, each with a one-line what-it-is.
- ❌ "Social media and blogging about various topics."
- ✅ "LinkedIn (primary) + short-form video. Pillars: **AI + your first job** (practical decoders), **access & the skills gap** (the frontline view), **field notes** (what I'm learning running programmes)."

---

## The synthesized outputs

- **Positioning statement** — "I help *[specific audience]* to *[specific outcome]* by *[your unique approach]*, because *[your purpose]*." Every slot must be fillable from the blocks above; if a slot is generic, a block upstream is still too weak.
- **Tagline** — short, memorable, and *only yours*. Offer 2–3; the person picks.
- **Content pillars** — 3–4, each with 2–3 example post/talk angles drawn from blocks 6 & 9.
- **Next actions** — concrete, this-week, doable. Not "post more" — "publish *[specific piece]* by Friday."
