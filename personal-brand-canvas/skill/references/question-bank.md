# Personal Brand Canvas — Question Bank

Per block: a **lead** question, a **probe ladder** (ask these when the answer is generic), and a **reflect-back** template. Adapt wording to the subject; never fire the whole ladder at once — probe only until the answer is specific.

---

## 1. Purpose & Values

- **Lead:** "When you're at your best in your work, what are you actually doing — and who is better off because of it?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "Why does that matter to *you*, personally?"
  - "What would you stand up for even if it cost you something?"
  - "Name 3 values you won't compromise — and for each, a moment you actually lived it."
- **Reflect:** "So your 'why' is *[…]*, anchored in *[value 1, 2, 3]*. Right?"

## 2. Vision & Goals

- **Lead:** "Fast-forward 3 years — what are you known for, and what's different in the world because of your work?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What's the 12-month version of that — the nearest concrete step?"
  - "What impact, *specifically*, and for *whom*?"
  - "How would you know you'd succeeded?"
- **Reflect:** "Near-term you're aiming at *[…]*; the longer arc is *[…]*."

## 3. Audience

- **Lead:** "Who do you most want to reach or serve? Picture one real person — describe them."
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What do they struggle with that you can genuinely speak to?"
  - "Where are they in their journey — just starting, stuck, scaling?"
  - "Who is this *not* for?" (narrowing is as important as naming)
- **Reflect:** "Your person is *[…]*, wrestling with *[…]* — and explicitly not *[…]*."

## 4. Unique Value

- **Lead:** "What can you offer that few others in your space can — your 'only-ness'?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What combination of things do you bring that rarely sit together?"
  - "What do people *thank you* for, specifically?"
  - "If you vanished, what would your audience miss that they couldn't easily get elsewhere?"
- **Reflect:** "Your edge is the intersection of *[…]* and *[…]* — few people stand there."

## 5. Strengths & Superpowers

- **Lead:** "What do people repeatedly come to *you* for?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What feels easy to you that others seem to find hard?"
  - "What's the evidence — a result, a moment, something people said?"
- **Reflect:** "Your superpowers: *[…]*."

## 6. Passions & Interests

- **Lead:** "What could you talk about for an hour with zero prep?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What makes you *angry* about your field? What makes you *hopeful*?" (emotion = content fuel)
  - "What are you learning right now just because you want to?"
- **Reflect:** "The energy is in *[…]*."

## 7. Story & Proof

- **Lead:** "Give me the short version of your journey to here — where's the turning point?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "What have you actually done that proves you can do this?" (roles, results, projects, artifacts)
  - "What's a specific, small, true detail that makes the story *yours*?"
- **Reflect:** "The arc: *[…]* → *[turning point]* → *[now]*, backed by *[proof]*."

## 8. Personality & Voice

- **Lead:** "Pick 3–5 adjectives for how you want to come across."
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "More formal or more playful? More bold or more measured?"
  - "Whose voice do you admire — and how would yours differ?"
- **Reflect:** "Voice: *[adjectives]*, leaning *[tone]*."

## 9. Channels & Content Pillars

- **Lead:** "Where does your audience already spend time — where will you actually show up?"
- **Probe ladder:**
  - "If you had to own 3–4 recurring themes, what are they?"
  - "What's one theme you'd deliberately *not* touch?"
  - "What format fits you best — writing, speaking, building, video?"
- **Reflect:** "You'll show up on *[channels]*, owning *[3–4 pillars]*."

---

## Probing discipline (the point of the skill)

- A generic answer ("I help people", "I'm passionate about tech") is a **prompt to probe**, never an answer to record.
- One good follow-up beats three shallow questions. Ask "*whom* / to do *what* / *how is that different*" until the answer could only describe this person.
- Reflect back in the subject's own words; if they wince at your phrasing, it's wrong — fix it.
